# Drengrfell

A drengr crosses a cold land of fells, forest, and ice. The ground is unmapped. Raise settlements and roads, take what the land yields, and keep moving while weather and the wild try to hold you still. The first hero to earn 20 points, or to raise 3 castles, wins.

## Turns

Play passes from one hero to the next. On your turn you may move, build, upgrade, place a works building, and trade with the bank. Enter or the End Turn button ends the turn.

On any turn you may move the camera, open the menus, claim a finished quest, and pay off a hazard.

At the start of your turn, each of your settlements may draw from the tiles around it, and the tile under your hero may pay as well. You begin with 7 wood, 7 wool, 7 stone, 7 grain, and 7 clay.

## Land and resources

| Ground | Yield |
|--------|--------|
| Forest | Wood |
| Grass | Wool |
| Mountain | Stone |
| Field | Grain |
| Clay | Clay |
| Water, ice | Nothing |

A resource tile pays by chance. Richer ground pays more often. When it pays, you receive 1. A works building you own on that tile raises its richness one step and pays 2 instead.

Weather changes when a turn ends.

- Snow makes every tile less likely to pay.
- Rain helps forest, grass, and field, and hurts mountain and clay.
- Sun helps mountain and clay.

## Move

Right-click a tile, then Enter or End Turn. The hero walks there and the turn ends. One walk per turn, up to 3 tiles. A marked path shows the route. Grass, field, and clay are easy. Forest is slower. Mountain and ice are harder. Water is slowest, so the hero goes around it when he can.

A hazard holds the hero in place. You can still end the turn.

Walking reveals the tiles along the path. Another hero is shown only on ground you have already seen.

## Build

Building, upgrading, and works all happen on your turn, and only if you can pay.

**Settlement** (N). A corner where tiles meet, beside your hero or beside a road. Not in open water, and not next to another settlement. Cost: 5 wood, 3 wool, 3 grain, 5 clay.

**Road** (B). An edge beside one of your settlements or one of your roads. Cost: 1 wood, 1 clay. Roads let you raise settlements far from the hero.

**Upgrade.** Click one of your settlements. A wooden hall becomes stone, and stone becomes a castle.

| Step | Cost | Points |
|------|------|--------|
| Stone | 10 wood, 10 wool, 20 stone, 10 grain, 10 clay | 1 |
| Castle | 30 wood, 20 wool, 50 stone, 40 grain, 30 clay | 4 |

A settlement raised all the way is worth 5 points. Three castles win the match.

**Works.** From the same settlement menu, on a neighbouring tile of the matching ground. One building per tile.

| Building | Ground | Cost |
|----------|--------|------|
| Lumber camp | Forest | 10 wood, 30 stone, 20 clay |
| Stable | Grass | 30 wood, 10 grain, 20 clay |
| Quarry | Mountain | 30 wood, 10 stone, 20 clay |
| Mill | Field | 20 wood, 20 stone, 20 clay |
| Kiln | Clay | 20 wood, 10 wool, 30 stone |

Press C to see settlement and road costs in the game.

## Trade

On your turn, T opens the bank. Give 4 of one resource and receive 1 of another. The two resources must differ. The bank is the only trader.

## Hazards

A hazard is rolled when you end your turn on dangerous ground, and only if you are not already held. It counts down when you end your own later turns. From the hazard notice you may pay it off on any turn. The price is the rate below times the turns still left.

| Ground | Hazard | Chance | Turns held | Pay per turn left |
|--------|--------|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Forest | Bear | 15% | 2 | 1 grain |
| Mountain | Rockslide | 20% | 3 | 2 wood |
| Clay | Sticky mud | 20% | 2 | 1 wood |
| Ice | Blizzard | 40% | 2 | 3 grain |

Grass, field, and water are safe.

## Quests

Q opens your quests. The tutorial quest is first. Finishing it unlocks three lines. Claim a finished quest for its resources on any turn. Each time a tile pays you counts once toward a gathering quest, even when a works building doubles the yield. Finishing every quest awards 5 points.

| Quest | Do this | Reward |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Foundation Stone | Finish the tutorial | 5 wood |
| New Frontiers | Build 3 settlements | 10 clay |
| Royal Arteries | Build 2 roads | 5 stone |
| Imperial Reach | Build 10 settlements | 5 stone |
| The Grand Network | Build 20 roads | 30 wood |
| Woodland Harvest | Be paid wood 7 times | 10 grain |
| Seasoned Veteran | End your turn 20 times | 5 wool |
| Stonemason's Pride | Be paid stone 30 times | 20 clay |
| Pathfinder | Discover 10 tiles | 20 stone |
| The Frozen Frontier | Reach ice | 10 wool |
| World Mapper | Discover 30 tiles | 25 wood |

Water does not count as a discovery.

## Win

20 points, or 3 castles. Points come from stone (1), a castle (4 more), and finishing every quest (5).

## Looking around

W A S D, or the edges of the window, move the map. Space centers the camera on your hero. The wheel, or + and −, zooms. V explains points. K lists the keys. J resets the tutorial.
